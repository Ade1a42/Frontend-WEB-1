Using class vs ID's
```text
Always keep IDs unique and reserve them for essential structural elements, JS targets, or internal section jump-links (like #about-me). Use Classes for grouped and reusable styling across multiple elements. If you want a specific element to stand out while keeping your overall design organized, combine base classes with unique, descriptive class names (e.g., class="widget first" or class="card card-featured") rather than misusing IDs for general visual styling.
```
Source: [HTML Classes and Ids – What They Are (And When to Use What)](https://torquemag.io/2018/02/html-classes-and-ids/)
